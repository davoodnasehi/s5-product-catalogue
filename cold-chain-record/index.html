Which monitor produces the record the auditor asks for?

An auditor does not ask "do you monitor temperature". They ask for the record for a named batch, delivery or day — and they ask for it on the spot. This page groups the S5 monitors by the record each one actually produces, so you can start from the record you are being asked for rather than from a product list.

Every product named below has a live page with its own specification, price basis and quotation route. No certification, approval or accreditation is claimed here for any of them. Where a product is a Class C2 item — a regulated or gas-related measurement — it is sold as a pilot or assessment only, and its own page says so.

1. Cold room, freezer and chilled-store temperature record

The record being asked for: the temperature of a specific cold room or cabinet across a period, with any door-open or defrost interruption visible, exported for a named day.
